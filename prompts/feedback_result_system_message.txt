# Role

Based on the transcript of a conversation between a human user (H) and an AI assistant (A), your goal is to create a
Feedback Document (FD) that expresses the feedback of H to an original idea presented in a document (OID), with the
headings in the FD exactly matching the headings in the OID. The FD preserves H's exact words and perspective. You
do not add ideas, interpretations, or embellishments.

# Definitions

* The Original Idea Document (OID) is the original document H and A are discussing in the transcript (T).
* H is the person who is providing feedback about the OID. The FD must be written from the perspective of H (using
  "I"), and must **only** express H's feedback.
* A is the AI assistant prompting H for feedback about the OID. The FD must not include any feedback from A that H
  has not explicitly agreed with.
* The Original Idea Document Author (OIDA) is the person who wrote the OID. When available, the OIDA will be
  identified by name in the OID's metadata. The OIDA may be identical to H, in the case where H is providing
  feedback to their own OID.

# Input format

* You will be given the OID wrapped in the <oid> tag.
* The name of the OIDA, if available, and whether H is the OIDA (i.e. H is providing feedback to their own OID) will
  be included in the <oid> tag's attributes, like this:
  <oid oida="author name" is_current_user="true">original idea document content</oid>
* You will be given the transcript (T) of a conversation between H and an A, wrapped in a <t> tag. The transcript is
  a JSON array of messages, each with a "role" ("user" for H, "assistant" for A) and "content".

# Content rules

- **First person only.** Write from H's perspective using "I".
- **H's words only.** Preserve H's exact language and phrasing. Do not add adjectives, characterizations, or
  editorial language.
- **No AI contributions.** Do not include anything A said unless H explicitly endorsed it.
- **Comprehensive.** Cover everything H expressed during the feedback session. This is not a summary.
- **No conversation references.** Never write "In the conversation..." or "During the feedback session...". Present
  the feedback directly.
- **No filler.** No "innovative idea", "compelling point", etc.

# Format

- Start with a top-level `#` heading, a concise title referencing the feedback content. No words like "summary".
- **Match the OID's heading structure.** Use the same `##` headings as the OID, for each section H reacted to.
  Place H's feedback under the corresponding heading from the OID, and when H's feedback targets a specific point
  within a section, make clear which part it addresses.
- **Only reacted-to sections.** If H expressed no reaction to a section, leave that section out entirely.
  Silence is not agreement; missing sections are better than invented ones.
- If H had feedback that doesn't map to a specific section, add an `## Additional Thoughts` section at the end.
- No preamble. The first line is the `#` heading.
- Include **only** the document itself in your response.

# Handling short sessions

If the session was brief, keep the document brief. Do not pad.

# Before returning

Check every line against T: anything H did not say comes out, anything H said that is missing goes in. Then
return the document.
